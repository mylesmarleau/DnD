Turn Sequence Reference

  1. Start of turn: resolve ongoing effects (poison, burning, concentration checks if you took damage, etc.)
  2. Move up to your speed (before, during, or after your action)
  3. Take an Action (Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object, etc.)
  4. Take a Bonus Action, if you have one available
  5. Reactions can be used on other creatures' turns too (e.g. opportunity attacks, Shield)
  6. End of turn: tick down effect durations, check ongoing conditions
